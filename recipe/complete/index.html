Homemade Cinnamon Rolls

Servings: 6-8
Prep Time: 30 min
Rise Time: 50 min
Bake Time: 20 min
Total Time: 1 hr 40 min

There’s nothing quite like the cherished tradition of eating homemade cinnamon rolls on Thanksgiving, Christmas morning, or other special occasions. With a pillowy soft dough (similar to our milk bread), a rich cinnamon filling, and melt-in-your-mouth cream cheese frosting, these Cinnamon Rolls are unbeatable!

Recipe contains gluten, dairy, and eggs.

Ingredients

    Dough

  • 1 cup warm milk (about 115°F)
  • 2 1/2 teaspoons instant dry yeast
  • 2 large eggs, room temperature
  • 1/3 cup salted butter, melted
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 4 1/2 cups bread flour

    • Filling

  • 1/2 cup salted butter, softened
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 2 tablespoons cinnamon
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream

    • Frosting

  • 6 ounces cream cheese, softened
  • 1/3 cup salted butter, softened
  • 2 cups powdered sugar
  • 1/2 tablespoon maple extract or vanilla

Tools/Equipment

  • Mixing bowls
  • Stand Mixer with Dough Hook (or use hands)
  • Rolling Pin
  • 9x13 baking pan
  • Towel/Wax paper
  • Cooking Spray

Instructions

Step 1: Preparing Dough

  1. Pour the warm milk into a bowl and sprinkle the yeast on top. Let it sit for 3–4 minutes until foamy.
  2. Add the eggs, melted butter, and sugar. Mix until combined.
  3. Add the salt and most of the flour. Mix until a soft dough forms. Let the dough rest for 5 minutes.
  4. Knead the dough for 5–7 minutes until smooth and stretchy. Add a little more flour only if needed.
  5. Place the dough in a greased bowl, cover it, and let it rise in a warm place until doubled in size (about 30 minutes).

Step 2: Filling and Shaping

  1. While the dough rises, mix the butter, brown sugar, and cinnamon for the filling.
  2. Roll the dough into a large rectangle on a floured surface.
  3. Spread the filling evenly over the dough.
  4. Roll the dough tightly into a log and cut it into 12 rolls.
  5. Place the rolls in a greased baking dish and let them rise for 20 minutes.

Step 3: Baking

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
  2. Warm the heavy cream slightly and pour it over the rolls.
  3. Bake for 25–30 minutes until lightly golden.

Step 4: Frosting and Storing

  1. To make the frosting, mix the cream cheese and butter until smooth.
  2. Add powdered sugar and extract, then spread frosting over cooled rolls.
  3. If there are leftovers, store at room temperature for 1-2 days or in the fridge for 3-5 days in an airtight container. To reheat, warm in oven at 350°F for 10-15 minutes or in the microwave for 10-30 seconds.
